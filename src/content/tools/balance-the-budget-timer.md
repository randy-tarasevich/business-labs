## How I use it

Built for **Budgeting on $2,400 a Month** (Microteach 1). Open it in a browser tab before class and switch to it at slide 8. Add `?embed=1` to the URL to hide the site header for projecting.

- **Warm-up (1:30):** sketch a spending plan for $2,400 after $800 rent.
- **Pair task (10:00):** Balance the Budget scenario cards. The current step highlights as time passes.
- **Exit ticket (3:00):** Google Form, voice level 0.

## Teacher cues

- The **halfway chime** at 5:00 is also the cue for planned check-ins with students who need them. Nothing about accommodations is shown on screen.
- **+1 min** is there if most pairs are on Step 4 when time runs low.
