## How I use it

Built for **Pay Yourself First: Building an Emergency Fund** (Microteach 2), the follow-up to Budgeting on $2,400 a Month. Same routine as the Balance the Budget Timer, so students already know how to read it. Open it in a browser tab before class and switch to it at slide 7. Add `?embed=1` to the URL to hide the site header for projecting.

- **Build the Fund (7:00):** home pairs on the balanced Month 2 scenario cards. Partner A calculates, Partner B checks against criteria 1 and 2. The halfway chime at 3:30 is the cue to switch roles for Part 2.
- **Move + Feedback (2:00):** Partner B moves clockwise. The traveler writes one fix on the peer slip, tied to a success criterion.
- **Emergency Strikes (5:00):** the new pair flips the month 4 Emergency card and writes a recommendation backed by a number.
- **Exit ticket (2:30):** Google Form from the slide QR code, voice level 0.

## Teacher cues

- Circulate in the order Card C, A, B, D during Build the Fund and mark the clipboard tracker: got it / almost / not yet.
- The halfway chime in each segment doubles as the cue for planned check-ins. Nothing about accommodations is shown on screen.
- **+1 min** is there if most pairs are still on Part 1 when the switch chime sounds.
