## How I use it

**Social Media Marketing, first metrics lesson.** 15–20 minutes. Comes before Influencer Deal Lab, which uses engagement rate to compare creators.

1. **Predict:** Project only the two follower counts: 50,000 and 4,000. Ask: "A local shop can pay one of these accounts to post about it. Which one?" Most students pick the bigger account.
2. **Test:** Load the big account, then the small one, and compare the results. Then pairs work the "Try it" questions.
3. **Explain:** Each pair writes two sentences to the shop owner: which account to pay, and the one number that proves it.

The bought-followers checkbox is the moment that sticks. Students know people buy followers; most have never thought about what it does to the math. Ask why a creator would do it anyway (it makes the account look bigger at a glance, and many people only look at the follower count).

The viral post is there to break the formula on purpose. When engagement by followers goes over 100%, students have to figure out why, which is how they learn what reach means.

## Answer notes

| Preset | Followers | Saw it | Interactions | By followers | By reach | Followers who saw it |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Big account | 50,000 | 4,000 | 250 | 0.50% | 6.3% | 8.0% |
| Big account + bought | 70,000 | 5,000 | 250 | 0.36% | 5.0% | 7.1% |
| Small local account | 4,000 | 2,500 | 320 | 8.0% | 12.8% | 62.5% |
| Small local + bought | 24,000 | 3,500 | 320 | 1.3% | 9.1% | 14.6% |
| Viral post | 1,200 | 90,000 | 8,550 | 713% | 9.5% | more than all |

- Bought followers: the tool adds 20,000 followers, shows the post to 1,000 of them, and none interact. That's a simplification. In real life, a sudden drop in engagement can also lead the app to show the account's posts to fewer real followers.
- Try it #4: 10% of 4,000 is 400 interactions, so the small account needs 80 more than this post's 320.
- This tool counts likes, comments, and shares equally. Many marketers count saves too, and some count only comments and shares as "real" engagement. Worth a quick discussion: which actions show more interest?

## Common misconceptions

- **"More followers means more people see every post."** Most followers never see a given post. The feed chooses what each person sees. The big account reaches only 8% of its followers.
- **"Engagement rate over 100% is a mistake."** It's the formula hitting its limit. When most viewers aren't followers, divide by reach instead.
- **"Likes are customers."** Engagement shows attention, not sales. Influencer Deal Lab and Marketing Funnel follow that attention through to money.
- **"Buying followers helps, it just doesn't help much."** It actively hurts the number brands check most, because the follower count grows and the interactions don't.
